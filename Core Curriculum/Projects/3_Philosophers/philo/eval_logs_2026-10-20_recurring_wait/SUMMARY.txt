WHY THE FOUR-PHILOSOPHER CASE NOW FAILS
In four_survival_1.log P1 starts eating at 600 ms, sleeps from 800 ms,
and logs thinking at 1000 ms. It dies at 1010 ms.
The recurring 100 ms wait can defer its next attempt to 1100 ms:
200 eating + 200 sleeping + 100 extra delay = 500 ms > 410 ms deadline.
This is a timing-budget problem even if forks were immediately available.
The condition tests odd ID, not odd philosopher count.
