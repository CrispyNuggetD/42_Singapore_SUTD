6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS / THINGS NOT PROVED

- Mandatory's deadlock prevention uses fixed ordering. Its relative waits encourage
  a pattern but do not establish strict scheduling fairness or a hard OS latency
  bound. The recorded 58/60 130 60 60 tight-margin experiments sometimes died.
  Do not call every such failure "impossible input": inspect fork/scheduling delay.
- Native evaluation observations were finite and used line-buffered file output.
  Logged deadline timing is not an independent proof of visible output latency.
- Race tools only cover observed executions; previous passing runs are evidence,
  not a proof of every interleaving.
- Bonus currently runs stubs: no real simulation, child hunger monitor, completed
  quota implementation or final logging protocol exists yet.
- wait_ms() has no process-wide stop/death check; a monitor/process-exit design
  must supply interruption. sem_wait can block; monitoring must still run.
- Bonus fixed names can remain after abrupt termination or an unlink failure.
  Failed close preserves its handle/name; failed unlink is reported after close.
  Child stop/reap failure returns before semaphore cleanup. Keep this distinction
  between attempted cleanup and guaranteed cleanup in explanations.
- Prior helper tests use substitute children and isolated semaphore names. Their
  successful results do not validate the unfinished run_philo_bonus routine.
